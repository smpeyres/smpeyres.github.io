I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Nuclear, Plasma, and Radiological Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I am grateful to be advised by Prof. R. Mohan Sankaran and funded by the DOE NNSA Stockpile Stewardship Graduate Fellowship.

I am broadly interested in electrons generated by low-temperature plasmas: their chemistry and transport, and the chemistry and transport they induce. I combine modeling and experiments to understand and control these processes.

I expect to defend my Ph.D. in March 2028. Afterward, I look forward to continuing this research in a government-lab, industrial, or academic setting, particularly in support of defense and national security missions.
